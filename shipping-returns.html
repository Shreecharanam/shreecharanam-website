Shipping & Returns

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Shipping across India

We ship to addresses across India.

Orders are usually dispatched within 1–2 working days.

Delivery generally takes 3–7 working days, depending on your location and the courier.

Shipping charges

Shipping is free on orders above ₹799.

For smaller orders, a small shipping charge applies — we confirm the exact amount with you on WhatsApp before dispatch.

Order confirmation & tracking

We confirm every order on WhatsApp before dispatch.

Once shipped, we share the tracking details with you on WhatsApp.

Returns & exchange

Because spices are food items, we are unable to accept returns on opened packs, for hygiene and safety reasons.

But if your parcel arrives damaged, leaking, short, or with the wrong item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We will replace it or refund you — your choice.

Cancellation & refunds

You can cancel an order any time before it is dispatched — just message us on WhatsApp.

Approved refunds are sent back through UPI or bank transfer within 5–7 working days.
