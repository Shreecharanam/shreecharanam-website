Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

What we collect

When you place an order, we ask for your name, phone / WhatsApp number, delivery address, and optionally your email and GSTIN.

The website itself does not create accounts and does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your basket stays only in your own browser until you send the order.

How we use it

We use these details only to: prepare your GST invoice, confirm your order on WhatsApp, and deliver the parcel to you.

We may contact you on the number you shared about your order (for example, to confirm an address or share tracking).

Who we share it with

We share your details only where it is needed to fulfil your order — for example with our courier partner for delivery, and for GST/tax compliance.

We never sell or rent your personal details to anyone.

How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept as long as required for accounting and tax purposes. After that, they are removed.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your details (other than records we must keep by law) by contacting us on the number or email below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
